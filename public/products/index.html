ACCESS AND BILLING

Understand how TeamPeach pricing works

Membership, credits and individual tool access are separate parts of the catalogue. Check the requirements for the tool you plan to use.

Does PeachBrief require a monthly membership?

No. PeachBrief can be used with available credits in your TeamPeach account. A recurring membership is not required to generate a brief. The brief is an AI-assisted editorial plan; it does not provide live search-volume, ranking or competitor data.

How does PeachChat use credits?

You can create, configure and test a PeachChat assistant with the free builder. AI replies use your account credits. Optional advanced features and conversation-saving controls are described on the PeachChat page.

What is the difference between a membership and a credit pack?

A membership is a recurring plan with its stated access and monthly credit allowance. A credit pack is a separate one-time top-up. Review each card for its current price, allowance, billing interval and eligibility before checkout.

Prices and available plans are loaded from the live catalogue. The cards above explain the access model; the catalogue itself remains the source for current amounts and payment options.
