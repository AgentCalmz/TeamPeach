BUSINESS RADAR

From a new name to a research lead.

Use domain wording to identify possible sectors and reasons to investigate further. Treat every signal as a starting point, not a verified company profile or a claim that a business needs a service.

01

Industry clues

Words such as “clinic”, “academy”, “shop” or “logistics” can help organise names into possible markets for further research.

02

Possible service fit

Some names may suggest a reason to explore website development, content, customer support or workflow automation opportunities.

03

Verify before outreach

Visit promising domains and check public evidence before treating them as businesses, deciding they are prospects or contacting their owners.

Evidence standard: the first release classifies wording found in domain names only. It does not yet verify ownership, live websites, company status, location, products, purchasing intent or service needs.

TeamPeach keeps the provider's meaning attached to each dataset. These signals should not be treated as interchangeable registration statuses.

01

Newly observed

Appeared in Webamon's newly observed zone-file data. This is not a confirmed registrar creation timestamp.

02

03

Expired

04

Dropped

Appeared in WhoisFreaks' free public dropped-domain list. A match still needs current registration verification.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Understand what the data can tell you.

Does a new domain mean a new business?

Not necessarily. It may represent a business, personal project, parked name, redirect or another use. Check the live website and other public evidence before drawing a conclusion.

Is the snapshot date the registration date?

No. It is the date of the source snapshot. Webamon records zone-file observations, not guaranteed registrar creation timestamps.

Does this use AI credits?

No. Loading the daily feed, searching, filtering and viewing name-based clues do not consume AI credits. AI-powered research or outreach features, when introduced, will be clearly identified before use.

Does a removed domain mean I can register it?

No. Removal from a monitored zone-file feed does not prove expiry or current availability. Verify with current RDAP data and a registrar.
