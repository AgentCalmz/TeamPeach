UNDERSTANDING THE RESULTS

Useful alerts, careful conclusions.

What makes a domain appear as a possible lookalike?

The scanner looks for exact brand-name matches, close spelling changes, common digit substitutions and names that combine a protected brand with other words. Some matches will be legitimate, unrelated or false positives.

What does “High priority” mean?

It means the name is very close to a protected brand or includes wording commonly associated with sign-in, accounts, verification, wallets or payments. It is a review priority, not a finding of fraud.

Are my brands saved to my TeamPeach account?

No. The current version stores your watchlist in the browser you are using. It is not synced to your account or to other devices. Clearing browser data can remove it.

Will I receive an email when a match is found?

Not in this version. Open the tool and run a scan against the latest available daily snapshot. Automatic monitoring and notifications are not included yet.

Does it check all registered domains?

No. It checks the latest newly observed domain feed available through TeamPeach. It does not cover every registered domain, every TLD or the full history of registrations.

What should I do when a match appears?

Visit the domain carefully, review current registration data through RDAP, preserve relevant evidence and consult qualified legal or security professionals before making an enforcement decision.
