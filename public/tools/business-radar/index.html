BUSINESS RADAR QUESTIONS

Understand what the research tells you.

What is Business Radar?

Business Radar is a domain-based business opportunity research tool. It scans newly observed public domain names for signals related to a business description, then helps you investigate selected websites using limited public homepage evidence.

Can Business Radar confirm a new company or sales prospect?

No. A domain appearing in a public feed does not prove a company has launched, is operating, owns the domain, needs a service or intends to buy. Treat every result as a lead for independent verification.

What website information does the AI review?

For up to five selected domains, TeamPeach checks reachability and sends a bounded sample of public homepage information, such as the page title, description and visible text. It does not crawl the entire website.

Does normal browsing consume AI credits?

No. Downloading and caching the public feed, filtering names and routine website checks do not use AI credits. AI intent ranking and selected-website research require active membership and credits.

Does the tool store every domain in TeamPeach's database?

No. The full compressed snapshot is cached in the visitor's browser and candidate matching runs locally. Only a bounded candidate list and selected homepage evidence are sent for the requested AI actions.

Can I export the research?

Yes. Completed website research includes a CSV export with the domain, match assessment, fit score, available website summary, supporting evidence, confidence notes and suggested next step.
