STATIC SAMPLE · NOT A LIVE RUN

Example: bookkeeping for a small business

Audience: first-time founders · Intent: informational · Format: practical guide

Suggested H1

Small Business Bookkeeping: A Practical Guide to Getting Started

Sample outline
  1. What bookkeeping tracks and why it matters
  2. Set up a simple chart of accounts
  3. Record income, expenses and receipts
  4. Reconcile records and review cash flow
  5. Common mistakes and when to ask an accountant
Questions to address

Which records should I keep? How often should I reconcile transactions? When is professional help worth the cost?
