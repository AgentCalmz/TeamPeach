BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Know what to expect.

TeamPeach publishes practical tools with explicit capability notes, so you can decide whether a ready-made tool fits or a custom build makes more sense.

Which tools are currently available?

The public catalogue currently lists the PeachChat — AI Customer Assistant and the PeachBrief — AI SEO Content Planner. We add other tools when they have a working public page and a clearly described purpose.

Does the PeachBrief — AI SEO Content Planner perform live SEO research?

PeachBrief uses DeepSeek to generate an editable, topic-specific content brief. It does not fetch live Google rankings, search volumes or competitor data, so review the outline against current search results and authoritative sources.

Does the AI chatbot automatically learn from my website?

No. You provide the business description, products or services, FAQs, policies and instructions. This keeps the assistant's source information under your control.

Can TeamPeach build something that is not listed?

Yes. Send a custom request describing the repetitive task, customer experience or workflow you want to improve. TeamPeach can discuss scope, requirements and a suitable build.
