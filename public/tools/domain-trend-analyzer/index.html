INTERPRETING THE DATA

Useful signals, not unsupported conclusions.

Does an increase mean more companies are being formed?

No. The counts represent domains appearing in monitored source feeds. One person or company can register multiple domains, and observations are not proof of a new operating company.

What does a rising extension mean?

It means that extension appeared more often in the latest available snapshot than in the previous available snapshot. It does not establish long-term popularity or market demand.

How are industry clues detected?

The browser checks domain-name words against a transparent set of sector-related terms. A category match is only a name-based clue. Categories can overlap and should be verified using independent evidence.

Does analysis use AI or my TeamPeach credits?

No. Daily charts and naming-pattern analysis are calculated in your browser from the public snapshots. No AI provider or TeamPeach credit balance is involved.
