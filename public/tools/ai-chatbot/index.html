What is the TeamPeach AI chatbot? It is a website assistant that answers visitor questions using the business information its owner supplies. It does not need to crawl the business website to get started.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know before you start.

Does TeamPeach automatically crawl my website?

No. The self-service chatbot uses the business information and instructions supplied by the owner. You do not need to provide a website or run a crawler.

Can I install one assistant on multiple websites?

Yes. One assistant can be installed on as many websites as you need. Domain restrictions are optional. When enabled, only the domains you list and the TeamPeach testing page are allowed.

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding is needed to create and configure the assistant. To install it, copy the universal HTML snippet and add it to your website template or page. If your platform does not allow custom scripts, contact TeamPeach support.

Are visitor conversations saved?

Free accounts do not store customer transcripts in TeamPeach. Premium members can choose to enable saving in their assistant settings, but the setting is switched off by default. A short conversation context stays in memory while that page is open. No visitor transcript is saved in TeamPeach unless the owner enables Premium conversation saving.

Does the assistant use my TeamPeach credits?

Yes. AI replies require available credits. DeepSeek usage is charged to the business owner's existing TeamPeach account credit wallet, using membership credits or purchased top-ups. Your dashboard shows chatbot requests, tokens and credits consumed.

Can the assistant make bookings or add products to a cart?

The self-service builder answers questions from owner-provided information. It does not make bookings, access customer orders or change a shopping cart. Those capabilities require a separately purchased and supported integration.

Can Nigerian businesses use the chatbot?

Yes. The hosted builder is designed for businesses including hotels, schools, retailers, restaurants, property businesses and service providers. It can be embedded on websites that allow a third-party HTML script.
