GOOD TO KNOW

Before you apply.

Where do these job listings come from?

PeachJobs indexes public job-posting APIs used by employers' career sites, including Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever. Each result links to its original listing.

Can a remote role still exclude Nigeria?

Yes. Remote jobs may be limited to specific countries or regions. Always confirm the employer's location requirements before applying.

Does PeachJobs upload my resume or apply for me?

No. PeachJobs does not ask for your resume or submit applications. You apply directly through the employer's original website.

Why might a listing no longer be available?

Employers can close roles at any time. PeachJobs tracks when listings were last observed, but the employer's page is the final authority on current availability.
